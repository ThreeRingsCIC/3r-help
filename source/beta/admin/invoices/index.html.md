This section contains all of your invoicing information.

At the top of the page you can see all of the invoices that have been raised for your organisation, and whether or not they have been paid.

Next you'll find boxes to enter your invoicing contact details, including your invoicing email address (optional), invoicing contact name, and invoicing postal address. You'll also need to add your organisation's annual turnover - because _Three Rings_ is only available to charities and other volunteer-powered non-profits, billing based on your turnover helps us to support as many organisations as possible.

[alert\_box type="info"]If you enter an invoicing email, we'll automatically send you electronic invoices, rather than sending them by post.[/alert\_box][alert\_box type="warning"]It's important that you enter accurate turnover in the Invoicing section: if we cannot accurately tell your organisation's turnover, you may be overcharged![/alert\_box]

At the bottom of the page, you can see how your postal address will appear when printed, allowing you to make any changes if needed. You'll see both your invoicing address, and (even if it's the same), the address we'll use to send you an annual Christmas card.

[alert\_box type="info"]Some organisations might not be individually invoiced, for example branches of a larger organisation might be invoiced as a group instead. If that's the case, you might not see all of the information and options described here.[/alert\_box]

If you have any questions about invoicing, please contact us on billing@threerings.org.uk

---
name: shift-history
title: Shift History
---

If you have the relevant permissions, the history of a shift, including who signed up, when they did so, and who's pulled out after signing up, can be viewed from the shift tab in the rota. Who can view the Shift History is controlled on a per-rota basis from Rota Permissions. By default, only roles with access to Admin -\> Logs panel can view this information. You can also compile a report of shift histories for multiple roots and dates in Stats.

Attachments that have been uploaded and sent through Comms are listed in the Attachments section.

[alert\_box type="info" class="corners"]Only attachments that are still available are listed - expired attachments can no longer be downloaded, even by their original recipients, and are not shown.[/alert\_box]

[alert\_box type="info" class="corners"]To reconfigure attachment settings, go to the [Security](/help/admin/security) section.[/alert\_box]

Each attachment that has been sent is shown on its own row, with various associated information and actions:

- **Date / Time -** the date and time the attachment email was sent
- **Sender -** who sent the email with the attachment
- **Recipients -** who the email was sent to. Click the number of recipients to see who they are.
- **Subject -** the subject line that was used for the email. Click the subject line to read the body text of the email.
- **File -** the file itself that was attached. Click the file name to download a copy and view.
- **Downloads** - the number of times the file has been downloaded. Click the number to see when it was downloaded, who downloaded it (if they were signed in), and their IP address at the time.
- **Security -** whether or not volunteers needed to be authenticated by signing into _Three Rings_ before being able to download the attachment.
- **Status -** whether the attachment is still available or if it has expired. You can also immediately expire the attachment, preventing any further downloads (even by the original recipient(s)) by clicking the Expire Now button.

[alert\_box type="warning" class="corners"]The Downloads column only lists downloads that were made by clicking or using the link in the attachment email. Administrators who download the file through this Attachments panel are not shown in this list, however these are shown in the [Logs](http://help/admin/log).[/alert\_box]

The Secrets panel allows you to see a list of all the secrets storeed in the secrets store, and who has seen them.

Secrets are displayed with their full filepath from the Filestore. You can find a secret by looking for it in the list, or selecting it from the drop-down list and then clicking the Filter button. Clicking the filepath will take you to that secret in the Filestore. Clicking a volunteer's name in the right hand column of the displayed list brings up a summary view of their provile with a link to their Directory page if needed.

You can also filter secrets by who has seen them. The default view shows any secret that's been seen by any volunteer, past or present. Using the drop-down list, you can choose to only show secrets that have been viewed by sleeping volunteers (i.e. volunteer accounts that have been slept, usually because they have left the organisation), or select any individual volunteer. Volunteers whose accounts have been slept and purged will be displayed with their volunteer ID only, otherwise they will be listed with their usual display name. Once you have selected an option from the drop-down menu, click the Filter button to display only the secret(s), if any, that have been viewed by that person. This can be helpful if a volunteer has left the organisation or changed role, for example, to know which codes or passwords may need to be changed.

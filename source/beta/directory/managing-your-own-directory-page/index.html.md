#### Editing your own Directory page

Navigate to your Directory page, either through directory view or by clicking on your username in the top-right hand corner of the page. Details can be changed by clicking 'edit' next to the field you wish to edit. Alter/Fill in the details, and then click `save`. Change complete the appropriate details, and then click the `Save Changes` button. #### Changing your password

Navigate to your Directory page, either through directory view or by clicking on your username in the top-right hand corner of the page. Locate the section labelled `Change Password`. In the first edit box, type your current password (to prove you are the user in question). In the second edit box, type what you want your new password to be. [alert\_box type="info" class="corners"] Your new password must be at least 8 characters long, and contain a mixture of letters and numbers.[/alert\_box] In the third edit box, type your desired new password again to confirm you typed it correctly, and then click `Change Password`. If the passwords match, then a yellow success message will be displayed at the top of the page and your password will have been changed. If the passwords do not match, or do not meet the requirements, then a red failure message will be displayed at the top of the page and your password will not have been changed. [alert\_box type="warning" class="corners"] You should **never** tell your _Three Rings_ password to anybody else, or use a password that you also use for anything else[/alert\_box]

#### Changing your photo

[alert\_box type="info" class="corners"] Uploading a new photo will automatically replace your old one, there is no need to delete your old photo first.[/alert\_box]

Navigate to your directory page and click the link labelled `Upload a new photo of yourself`. Click the 'choose file' button and use the popup window to locate the new photo of yourself that you wish to upload. When you find the photo that you wish to upload, double-click on it and click the `Upload` button. You will be invited to crop the photo to decide which portion of it should be displayed in the button view - move the window so that it shows the appropriate part of the photo (usually your face!).

#### Taking a photo with a camera

You can take a new photo for _Three Rings_ using your camera and add it to your Directory page immediately. To do this, go to your Directory page, select `Upload a new photo of yourself` and select `take photo `you may need to enable your camera by clicking in the displayed menu). A preview will be displayed - click on `take photo` when you are happy with the picture displayed. You can then crop the picture (if you wish) and click `OK` and the picture will be uploaded. To delete a picture, click the link labelled `delete current photo`.

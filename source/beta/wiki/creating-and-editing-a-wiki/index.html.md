#### Creating a Wiki

Click on the wiki tab 'Wiki' at the top of the page and then click the 'Create Wiki' button at the bottom of the page. Enter the name of the new wiki in the edit box.

[alert\_box type="warning" class="corners"]Once chosen, wiki names **can't be changed**.[/alert\_box]

Press the `Create Wiki` button.

#### Creating a Wiki Page

To create a Wiki page, navigate to a page in the specific Wiki you want the page to be in. If it's a Wiki you've just created, this will be the Index page. Click `Edit` and then type in two capitalised words next to each other without a space - for example, 'OpenDay' or 'TrainingNotes'. Scroll down, click `Save Changes`, and when the page refreshes, this will now be a brown link with a question mark next to it. If you click on this link, you'll be taken to a page with those two words as a title. Clicking `Edit` will allow you to add content.

#### Wiki Editing Tools

There are several tools that allow you to apply different effects to the text on your wiki pages. Using these tools will insert formatting characters such as ! and \* into your text, which will only show up as the desired formatting. If you are unsure that they are being applied at any point, click the `Save Changes` button, which will allow you to see the correct, formatted version of the page. Then you can switch back to editing by clicking the `Edit` link near the top of the page again. It's not possible to upload files into Wikis, but you can add in images by providing links to them.

**Creating Headings**

Type the text you wish to make a heading then highlight it and click the drop-down box labelled displaying `Paragraph`. A menu will appear with a variety of different headings in it, numbered 1-6. 1 is the biggest and 6 the smallest. Click the `Save Changes` button when you are finished altering the page and the change will be applied.

title: Logging in for the first time
---

The first time you log into _Three Rings_ you'll use a slightly different process to the one you'd use afterwards. In particular, you'll be prompted to use a new password after you've logged in. The password given in your Welcome Message is a Temporary Password it will only work the first time you log into _Three Rings_, and afterwards you'll need to use your newly chosen password. [alert\_box type="info"] When your _Three Rings_ account is first created, it's considered 'Active' (even before you've logged in). Active accounts may still be affected by what others do on _Three Rings_ - for example, you may be signed up to shifts by Rota managers, and may receive emails sent through the Comms system, even without logging in)[/alert\_box] If you have an email address, and have given it to your organisation, you may receive your Welcome Message by email, or on paper. If you haven't given an email address to your organisation, you will receive the Welcome Message on paper. (You don't have to have an email address to use _Three Rings_!). Your Welcome Message will tell you what your _Three Rings_ username is, and give you a Temporary Password to log in with. [caption id="attachment\_2761" align="aligncenter" width="1024"] [![An example of the Welcome Message for a fictional volunteer](https://www.threerings.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2016/04/Welcome-Message-1024x568.png)](https://www.threerings.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2016/04/Welcome-Message.png)

This example of a Welcome Message tells the fictional volunteer all they should need to know to get started with _Three Rings_[/caption] Once you've successfully logged in, you'll be prompted to change the Temporary Password into something which you can remember. [alert\_box type="warning"] You should **never** tell your _Three Rings_ password to anybody else, or use a password that you also use for anything else.[/alert\_box] Once you've logged in with a temporary password, you'll be asked to change it[/caption] Type the Temporary password into the 'Current password' box, and type your new password into the 'New Password' boxes, then click 'Change Password'. Once you've clicked 'Change Password', you'll be taken to the [Overview page](https://www.threerings.org.uk/help/overview/) - in future, you'll log in with the password you've just set, and you'll be taken straight to the Overview page.

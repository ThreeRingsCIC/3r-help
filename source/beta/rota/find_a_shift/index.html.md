title: Upcoming Swaps and Gaps
---

You can view a list of upcoming gaps that you could sign up to. You can change what dates to look for using the date boxes at the top, and which Rota or Rotas you want to find shifts on using the tickboxes. Once you change the dates and/or Rotas selected, click Refresh to update the list of available shifts.

First, you will see a list of understaffed shifts - these are shifts that need one or more volunteers to reach minimum staffing. Next, you'll find a list of shifts where another volunteer has requested a swap. Finally, you'll find a list of optional shifts, where the minimum staffing level has been reached but there are still spaces available for additional volunteers.

To sign up for one or more shifts, tick the checkbox(es) next to the shift(s) you want to sign up for, then click Sign Up.

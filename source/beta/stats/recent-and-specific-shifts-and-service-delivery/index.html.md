## Most Recent Shifts

'Most Recent Shifts' reports on when volunteers undertook their most recent shift. Clicking on the `Most Recent Shifts` link will display a table showing, for each rota, the date of each volunteer's last shift, and their next shift. You can sort the table by any of the columns by clicking on any of the column titles; for example clicking on `Volunteer` will sort the table records by volunteer name, in alphabetical order. To reverse the order, click on the same column again. If you have Comms permissions, you can use the checkboxes by each volunteer to send a message to them Directly: just click the Send Email or Send SMS button to be taken to the Comms page to [compose your message](/help/comms/writing-a-message-in-comms/).

## Specific Shifts

The 'Specific Shifts' option produces reports on which shifts volunteers have filled in the past. To generate a report, click the `Stats` tab at the top of the page, and then the `Specific Shifts` link. You will have the option of selecting dates between which to display shifts by clicking on the calendar buttons. Click on the right arrow to move the calendar forward one month at a time, and click on the left arrow to move it back one month at a time. Clicking on a shift in the table will display the number of volunteers who have staffed the shift you selected in the past, as well as the number of times they have done so. [alert\_box type="info"]After you have selected a shift, two further date edit boxes will appear with which you can change the period the report covers.[/alert\_box]

## Service Delivery Reports

The 'Service Delivery' option gives overall statistics on how many scheduled shifts are run. To access these statistics, click the `Stats` tab at the top of the page, and then the `Service Delivery` link. The system will automatically show statistics for the past four weeks across all rotas. You can change the time period of the statistics by using the two calendar buttons at the top of the page, selecting the dates between which you wish to view statistics, and clicking `Refresh`. You can change the shifts covered by the statistics by ticking or unticking the relevant checkboxes, and clicking `Refresh`.

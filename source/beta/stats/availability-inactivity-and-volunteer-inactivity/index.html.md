---

#### Availability

To access summary information on when your organisation's volunteers are available during the week, click on the `Stats` tab and the `Availability` link. This page will display a table of volunteers' availability by day and hour. A blank entry means that all volunteers are available. A red cross with a number indicates that that many volunteers are unavailable. Clicking on the number will list the names of the unavailable volunteers.

#### Inactivity

The Inactivity tool displays when your volunteers have scheduled periods of inactivity over the next three months. To see this, click the `Stats` tab and the `Inactivity` link. This will display a calendar with the number of volunteers who have scheduled periods of inactivity, split by inactivity types set for your organisation. A blank entry means no volunteers have scheduled periods of inactivity. The `Previous 12 weeks` and `Next 12 weeks` buttons allow you to change the time period covered. The `PDF`, `CSV`, and `Excel` links at the bottom of the calendar will allow you download the information in the specified format.

#### Volunteer Inactivity

The Volunteer Inactivity tool will display information on which volunteers are inactive during a specified period, for how long, and between which dates. To access this information, click on the `Stats` tab and the `Volunteer Inactivity` link. You can change the dates covered by using the calendar buttons and clicking the `Refresh` button.

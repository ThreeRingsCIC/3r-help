_Three Rings_ has been designed to be as user-friendly as it can be, but it is still possible to get stuck sometimes. If you have problems using _Three Rings_, here's what you should do:

## Speak to your local Support People

The first people to speak to if you're having trouble with _Three Rings_ are the designated Support People at your organisation. If you don't know who they are, you can find out by clicking the 'Help' link in the top right corner of _Three Rings_ when you're logged in. Click `'Click here for more help'` link at the bottom of the box that pops up, and you'll be taken to a page with the contact details of all of the Support People at your organisation. If you're already logged in, you can [see who they are now by clicking here](/help/support).

## Contact Three Rings support

If your Support People can't help you, if you don't know who they are, or if you are a Support Person and need more help, get in touch with the _Three Rings_ Support team. You can do this by [filling in the contact form](http://www.threerings.org.uk/contact) or by emailing [support@threerings.org.uk](mailto:support@threerings.org.uk). We'll do our best to answer you within a maximum of 24 hours. To help us, please remember to tell us:

- Your Three Rings username ( **do not send your password!** ) and the name of the organisation you volunteer with
- What you're doing when things go wrong - walk us through the steps: tell us what you click on/type in in what order
- What happens - it's easier for us to tell what's going wrong if you explain _how_ something isn't working: for example, are you seeing an error message (and what does it say?), or is part of the system not doing what you expected (and what did you expect it to do)?
- If you're using the Contact Form, please make sure you've typed your email address correctly!

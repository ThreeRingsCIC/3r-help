The About Your Organisation tool allows you to share various pieces of information with volunteers in your organisation, and potentially with volunteers in other, affiliated organisations. [alert\_box type="alert" class="corners"]The About Your Organisation tool can, if used incorrectly, allow secret information such as perhaps, your organisation's internal phone number, to be shared with volunteers in other organisations. As such, this tool should be used with care. If in any doubt about how to use this tool, please contact a Support Person at your organisation, or contact the _Three Rings_ support team on support@threerings.org.uk.[/alert\_box]

#### Details that can be Shared

- `Address` - The address from which your organisation operates.
- `Admin number` - The admin line number for your organisation (if you have one)
- `Contact role` - The person(s) within the organisation that should be contacted with official enquiries. (Selected by nominating)
- `Directions` - Details of how to get to the organisation's premises
- `Door Code` - The code to gain access to the office, where applicable.
- `Email` - The email address for **service users** to contact the organisation
- `Face to Face` - Allows you to provide details about any Face-to-Face services your organisation provides to service users.
- `Facebook` - The web address of your organisation's Facebook page.
- `Google+` - The web address of the organisation's Google+ page.
- `Hotline Number` - The number service users can ring to contact your organisation.
- `Hours open for Visitors` - The times your organisation is for face-to-face visitors
- `Instant Messenger` - Details of how service users can contact your organisation by instant message.
- `Linkedin` - The address of your organisation's Linkedin page.
- `Organisation Email` - The e-mail address your **volunteers** should use to contact the organisation.
- `Office Staffed Hours` - The hours your organisation is usually staffed.
- Other - Allows details of other services to be listed.
- `Out Of Office` - Allows details about 'out of office' services to be provided.
- `Postal Mail` - The postal address for service users.
- `Prison` - Details of any prisons supported by your organisation.
- `SMS` - The SMS number for service users to contact your organisation.
- `Telephone` - Details about the telephone service provided by your organisation.
- `Twitter`- The official Twitter account name of your organisation.
- `Website`- The website address of your organisation. 

#### Privacy levels

- `Nobody` - No-one can see the information (apart from Administrators, when using this tool)
- `Org` - This information can be seen by volunteers at your organisation.
- `Parent` - Volunteers at your organisation, and your parent organisation (if you have one) can see this information.
- `Siblings` - Volunteers at your organisation, and your parent organisations (if you have one) and other associated organisations can see this information.
- `Ancestors` - Volunteers at your organisations, and your parent organisations (if you have them) can see this information.
- `Family` - This information can be seen by volunteers at your organisations, as well as by volunteers in other organisations affiliated with your organisations.
- Public - This information can be shared with the public. If you have a public webpage on _Three Rings_, it may be visible there.

[alert\_box type="info" class="corners"]Many Privacy Levels operate in relation to your Organisation's family within _Three Rings_ - if your organisation is not part of a "family" of other similar groups (for example, a Samaritans branch within a Samaritans region), information will not be shared with anyone, even if a sharing setting is selected.[/alert\_box]

#### Adding a Detail

Click on the `Add Detail` button at the top of the page and select the detail to be added from the drop down list. Click the Add button and enter the information in the edit field for the new detail. Lastly, click the `Save Changes` button. To edit a detail, locate the correct detail in the tables. Change the contents of the detail's edit box and click the `Save Changes` button. [alert\_box type="info" class="corners"] The Organisation, Service and Volunteer details have different tables.[/alert\_box] To delete a detail, locate the correct detail in the tables and click the `Delete` button.

#### Changing a Privacy Level

Locate the correct detail in the tables and click the current privacy level of the detail (located in the first column of each table, marked **Privacy** ). From the pop-up box, click the radio button next to the new desired privacy level. Click `OK` and then `Save Changes`.

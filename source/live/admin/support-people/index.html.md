This page shows you who the current Support People at your organisation are and explains what the role involves. It also allows you to give this role to anyone else who is eligible. In order to eligible, the user has to have Directory and Manage permissions. You can have more than one Support Person.

To nominate a new Support Person, scroll down to the Nominate Support People section. Select the name of the volunteer from the drop-down menu, and click the Make Into Support Person button.

Support people:

- Will have their name and primary contact details listed on the [Help-\>Support](/help/support) page, for all of your volunteers to see when they get stuck with _Three Rings_, even if those contact details normally aren't visible.
- Don't need to be computer experts, but should have a good problem-solving and analytical attitude, as well as an interest in helping their fellow volunteers get around _Three Rings_.
- Will be the first point of contact for your volunteers when they have trouble with _Three Rings_. If your Support People are also stuck, they can get in touch with the _Three Rings_ Support Team for further help.

[alert\_box type="info"]Your Support People will be the first port of call when your volunteers need technical support - and if your volunteers need a password reset or we need to communicate with your organisation, they're the people we'll be in touch with. You should always have at least one Support Person[/alert\_box]

To remove a Support Person, find their name in the list of Support People at the top of the page, and click the Remove button under their name and details.

[alert\_box type="warning"]Support Person status will be automatically removed from a volunteer if they become ineligible, e.g. because they lose the Directory: Manage permissions by losing the relevant role or the role itself changing.[/alert\_box]

As well as Support People, you can also nominate other organisational contacts. If Three Rings needs to get in touch with your organisation, we'll normally contact your Support People. However, you can nominate a Role that would be better to contact for certain circumstances:

- Roles with decision-making authority would be contacted about things like updates to our Terms and Conditions, or to make you aware of a data protection issue.
- Roles with technical authority would be contacted about things like configuration issues with _Three Rings_ or system updates that might affect you.

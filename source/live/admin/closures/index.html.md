Closures are used to stop shifts on a rota for a period of time, and can be set by any volunteer with Admin privileges. The most common reasons to introduce closures are for holiday periods or times when your premises are undergoing refurbishment.

[alert\_box type="warning"] Setting a closure will either **delete** or **close** all shifts during the period of closure. Volunteers won’t be able to sign up to any shift on the affected rota(s) during the closure.[/alert\_box]

## Viewing and Editing defined closures

Existing Closures can be viewed in the table, and details of these closures can be altered using the "Edit" button.

## Adding a new closure

To create a new closure, enter a description in the first field. Enter a `Start date` and for the closure and either tick the checkbox to make the closure `Apply to all rotas` or select a single rota to which the closure applies. Use the dropdown to specify whether shifts in the Closure period should be closed or deleted. Select whether you want the shifts during the closure to be closed or deleted. Click the Add Closure button to create the Closure.

[alert\_box type="warning"]Once a Closure has been created, deleting the Closure will **not** reopen or restore closed and deleted shifts within the Closure period. New shifts will not be created automatically during the Closure.[/alert\_box]

When creating a closure you will be asked to confirm your choice to close or delete all shifts affected by the closure.

## Closing multiple rotas

To close more than one, but less than all, of your Rotas you will need to create multiple closures for the same period, one for each separate Rota you wish to close.

## Editing and Deleting closures

**Editing** a closure will not re-open or re-create any shifts affected by the original closure, but will allow you to adjust its dates or description. To do this, locate the closure you wish to edit in the table of closures at the top of the page, and click the `Edit` button. When you’ve finished making your changes, click the ` Save Closure` button. **Delete** a closure by finding it in the table of closures at the top of the page, and clicking the `Delete` button. Once you have confirmed that you wish the closure to be deleted, it will be removed.

---

To log into _Three Rings_: Go to www.3r.org.uk

1. Type your username and password into the appropriate boxes
2. Click on the Log In button

If you have any problems with logging in, the best person to contact is the support admin at your local organisation. If you aren’t sure who this is, e-mail us at [support@threerings.org.uk](mailto:support@threerings.org.uk) and we can let e-mail them on your behalf.

#### Tips

- You can go to [3r.org.uk](http://www.3r.org.uk/) either by clicking on the link or by typing in into the address bar at the top of your web browser: note that searching using Google or other search engines may not find [www.3r.org.uk](http://www.3r.org.uk/).
- You can set [3r.org.uk](http://www.3r.org.uk/) as your homepage or add it to your favourites/bookmarks list in your web browser, to make it easier to get there next time. Consult your web browser help for information on how to do this.
- You'll need to type your username and password exactly right: in your password, it even matters whether or not the letters are upper or lower case, so you might need to check that your Caps Lock is turned off!

### 'Login Failed' message

If you see the error message:

`Login failed Bad username/password combination`

it means that either the username, or the password, which you entered was incorrect. For security reasons, _Three Rings_ cannot reset your password for you: if you have a self-managed account, you may be able to perform a password reset yourself (depending on your security settings). If you do not have a self-managed account, you will need to speak to one of the Support People at your organisation - if you don't know who these people are, e-mail us at [support@threerings.org.uk](mailto:support@threerings.org.uk) and we can let them know on your behalf.

### Logging Out of _Three Rings_

When you’re done using _Three Rings_, click the link on the upper-right corner of the page, to protect your account.

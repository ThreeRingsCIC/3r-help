#### Locking and Unlocking a Wiki

If you want to stop your volunteers making any more changes to a wiki, you can lock it and people will only be able to view it without making changes. This can be undone by unlocking the Wiki at any time.. [alert\_box type="info" class="corners"] Your role must have wiki: manage permissions in order to lock or unlock a wiki.[/alert\_box] Click on the wiki tab at the top of the page and then click the `Lock` link under the name of the wiki you want to prevent further changes to. To unlock a locked Wiki, click the `unlock` link under the name of the wiki you now want to allow changes to.

#### Deleting a Wiki

[alert\_box type="danger"]Deleting a wiki permanently removes its entire contents. Deleted wikis cannot be recovered - once they're deleted, they're gone! There's **no** recycling bin for wikis.[/alert\_box] Click on the wiki tab `Wiki` at the top of the page. Click the `delete` link under the name of the Wiki you wish to delete then click `OK` on the popup box that appears.

[alert\_box type="info"]Deleting the WikiWord that links to a Wiki doesn't delete the Wiki itself. If a new link is created with the same WikiWord, it will link to the existing wiki.[/alert\_box]

---

[alert\_box type="info"] In order to view a Wiki, you have to have the relevant permissions. If you can't see the Wiki tab, it's likely that either you don't have the relevant permissions, or your organisation hasn't enabled the Wiki feature.[/alert\_box]

Click on the Wiki tab 'Wiki' at the top of the page. You'll be taken to a list of Wikis that are currently stored. Clicking 'Random page' will take you to a random page, or you can select a specific one by clicking on it's name. This latter option will take you to the Index Page of the wiki. To view other pages of the wiki, click on the 'All Pages' link, and then click on the name of the page you want to view from the list to display that page. To get back to the main menu of Wikis again, click on the 'Wiki' tab up at the top.

#### Navigating Between Wiki Pages

To navigate between pages in a Wiki, you use the links within the Wiki text. Links are underlined and coloured brown. There are two types of links - links to pages, and links to pages that have yet to be created. Those that aren't created have little question marks between them. If you click on one without a question mark, you'll be taken to that existing page. If you click on one with a question mark, you'll be taken to a blank page where you can then fill out content (if you have permissions to edit the Wiki).

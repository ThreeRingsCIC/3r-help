#### Putting a volunteer's account to sleep

Putting a volunteer's account to sleep can be used to remove a volunteer from the organisation. This can be done on a temporary basis, or when someone is permanently leaving (by clearing their personal data). Go to the volunteer's Directory page through directory view. Click `edit` at the top of the page then locate the section entitled `Account Status` Then click the `Sleep account` button. [alert\_box type="info" class="corners"] To wake an account that has been put to sleep, repeat the above steps, but click the button labelled `Wake account`. You may need to filter the Directory to show Sleepers to find their Directory page [/alert\_box] [![Deleting accounts](https://www.threerings.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2016/04/Deleting-accounts-236x300.png)](https://www.threerings.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2016/04/Deleting-accounts.png)

#### Deleting an account

To permanently delete an account, follow the above steps, but before clicking `sleep account`, tick the box labelled `Clear personal information`. The username associated with this account will be freed up within 24 hours. [alert\_box type="danger"] **WARNING:** Clearing personal information will permanently and irrevocably remove **all** information about the volunteer from the Three Rings system - it cannot be undone.[/alert\_box]

#### Locking a volunteer's account

Locking a volunteer's account is used to prevent them from logging in. Navigate to the volunteer's directory page through directory view. Click `Edit` at the top of the page and locate the section entitled `Account Status`. Click the `Lock account` button. [alert\_box type="info" class="corners"] To unlock an account, repeat the steps above, but click the button labelled `Unlock` [/alert\_box]

Concurrent Volunteers

The Concurrent Volunteers report shows how many volunteers are on duty at any one time. It is mostly useful if there are multiple rotas shifts but you want to know how many volunteers are active.

![](https://www.3r.org.uk/inline_images/faccb2b4-4e06-4587-9ee7-99dbe1649e79)

Volunteer Hours

The Volunteer Hours report shows how many volunteer hours were accrued by hour throughout the day. So, if three volunteers were on shift from 13:00 - 15:30, it would show 3 volunteer hours for 13:00 - 14:00 and 14:00 - 15:00, and 1.5 volunteer hours for 15:00 - 16:00 (because they were only on duty for half an hour).

![](https://www.3r.org.uk/inline_images/a7c090ad-2a39-4147-9e47-a29c227fb0e1)

Daily Manifest

Use the Daily Manifest report to list the volunteers on duty on a particular day - optionally together with their photos.

![](https://www.3r.org.uk/inline_images/51ca0ee8-9ac1-41b2-b282-7df561a04ccd)

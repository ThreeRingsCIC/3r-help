Account Information

This report provides useful information about your volunteers: when they last logged in, whether their email accounts are verified or blocked, whether they have opted out of email or text messages (if used), and whether they have subscribed to the Weekly Digest.

It is your first stop if a volunteer is having trouble receiving emails. Check that their account is not blocked and that they haven't opted out of receiving emails from _Three Rings_. If it is blocked, ask them to email support@threerings.org.uk from the affected account so the support team can investigate the reason.

If they have opted out of receiving emails, they need to go to their account page, select Comms Notification Options and tick the relevant box.

If a volunteer is labelled as having No account, they cannot log in because their _Three Rings_ account has been deleted. If they are still an active volunteer, you can recreate their account with a new username and password. This returns self-managed accounts to organisation-managed status.

Recurring Shifts

Lists all your recurring shifts giving the times, start and finish dates, and frequency. This is intended as a debugging report primarily for Three Rings staff. It doesn't translate odd repeat frequencies (because interpreting them was too complicated!)

Something like 'Complex: EveryTE start: 2021-05-09 interval: 14 precision: !ruby/object:Runt::DPrecision::Precision precision: 3' means the shifts repeat at 14-day intervals - but there are other more complicated possibilities too!

Contact Tracing

Reports on who was on duty with whom just in case there is a pandemic...

Personal Information

Provides a list of data held by _Three Rings_ relating to a single volunteer - it can get quite long. Might be useful if you have to respond to a Subject Access Request (SAR) request.

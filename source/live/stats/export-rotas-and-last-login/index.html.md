## Export Rotas

The 'Export Rotas' tool lets you present your rota, and the volunteers filling shifts, in a list format. To access this tool, click on the `Stats` tab at the top of the page, and then on the `Export Rotas` link. You have the option of selecting a rota to display using the drop-down menu, and to edit the dates covered by clicking on the calendar buttons. Clicking the `Generate Report` button will automatically generate a list of shifts under the selected rota(s) and for the selected dates. You can download the list as a CSV file by clicking `Download as CSV`.

## Roles

This report lists which volunteers at your organisation have which Roles. You can sort the list either by the names of the Roles, the names of the volunteers, or the dates on which Roles were assigned to volunteers. In addition to viewing this report online, you can optionally download it as a PDF, or as a .csv or .xls spreadsheet: links to do this appear below the report.

## Volunteer Snapshot

The Volunteer Snapshot report shows how many volunteer accounts were awake at a particular date in the past. Of course, some of those people will have left since some date in the past - if personal information has been deleted they will be identified only by number.

title: Viewing Sent Messages
---

Scroll down to the bottom of the page and click the `View Sent Messages` link. Sent messages will be displayed in a list. To view all the details of a sent message, click on it. To switch back to sending messages in Comms, click the link near the top of the page.

[alert\_box type="info"]_Three Rings_ stores sent messages on its own server - they're not kept on your local device, and don't take up any space.[/alert\_box]

If you want, you can delete a sent message: just click the 'Delete' button beside the message you want to get rid of, and it will be removed from the list. Note that deleted messages can't be brought back!

[alert\_box type="warning" class="corners!]Deleted messages can't be brought back, but the original record of the message that was sent will still be stored in Admin \> Logs[/alert\_box]

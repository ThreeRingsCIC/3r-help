The Overview is the first page most volunteers will see when they log into _Three Rings_ It shows what's happening in your organisation, including news items, upcoming events, and details about your organisation. Admins can restrict who can see the Overview page based on their role.

[alert\_box type="info"]To make changes to the information on the Overview page, your organisation needs to grant you a Role that gives you Manage or Self Manage permissions for News and Events[/alert\_box]

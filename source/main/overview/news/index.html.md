The news feature is a great way to share information with your volunteers. By default, newer items appear at the top of the News, and older ones lower down, banded together based on their Priority though Sticky items come before non-Sticky items . News items will disappear when they are edited, archived, or deleted. You can set an Archive Date as far in advance as you want.

**[![News Items](https://www.threerings.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2016/04/News-Items-300x209.png)](https://www.threerings.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2016/04/News-Items.png)**

## Adding a News Item

To add a News item, click the `Add a news item +` link near the top of the page. This will open the News Editing box. Once you've provided a Title and a Body for the news item you can choose whether or not to make it 'Sticky' by checking the `Sticky?` checkbox. Sticky news items remain at the top of the news feed. News items will have a default Archive Date though you can change it using the archive date selector.

## Editing News Items

You can Edit an existing News Item by clicking the `Edit` button next to the Title. Make any changes necessary to the Title or Body of the News item and click the `Update` button to save changes. At this time you can also toggle whether or not the item is Sticky, and set or adjust its auto-archive date.

#### Editing the Priority of News Items

It's also possible to adjust existing the _priority_ of existing News items. News items can be assigned a priority of **High** , **Medium** , or **Low**. Items with higher priorities will appear above those with lower priorities, even if a lower-priority News item was created or edited more recently. All News items are created with Medium priority by default. [alert\_box type="info" class="corners"]Setting all of your News priorities to the same level effectively cancels out their priority setttings![/alert\_box] To adjust the priority of a News Item, click on the triangular 'expand' icon (`►`) next to the `Edit` button. This will expand the Editing tools. You can increase a News Item's priority by clicking the up arrow (`↑`), and decrease it by clicking the down arrow (`↓`).

#### Minor Edits

You can make minor edits to News Items. These edits will still appear in the logs, a minor edit means it won't bump the post up to the top like making a normal edit would. It also prevents the edit from showing up in the Weekly Digest. To do this, click `Edit` on the News Item you want to change, make the changes you want and then make sure to tick the box next to `Minor edit?` before clicking `Update`.

## Archiving and Deleting News Items

A News Item will be given a default Archive date though you can manually Archive or Delete it. To do this, click on the triangular 'expand' icon (`►`) next to the `Edit` button. You will see two buttons, `Archive` and `Delete`. Clicking on either button will prompt you to confirm your choice; confirming will Archive or Delete the News Item depending on which button you clicked. [alert\_box type="alert"]Archiving or Deleting a News Item removes it from the Overview for **every volunteer** at your organisation! Deleted News items can't be brought back, so it's usually better to Archive old News rather than Delete it.[/alert\_box]

#### Managing Archived News Items

If you need to display a News Item that's been Archived, you can do this by going to the bottom of the Overview and clicking `Archived News...` This will show a list of all Archived News Items at your organisation. (If your Organisation has no Archived News, the `Archived News..`. link will not appear). You can restore an Archived News Item to the Overview page by clicking the `Unarchive` button and confirming your choice, or you can Delete it forever by clicking `Delete` and then confirming your choice.

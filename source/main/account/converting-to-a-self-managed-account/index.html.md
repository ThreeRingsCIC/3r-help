### What is a Self-Managed account?

By default, _Three Rings_ accounts are managed by the organisation they are created by. However, users may find it beneficial to elect to manage their own accounts, especially if they volunteer with more than one organisation that uses _Three Rings_.

### Changing your account to be self-managed

[alert\_box type="warning" class="corners"]Once you change your account to be self-managed, you **cannot easily change it back again** at a later time.[/alert\_box] [![Managed Accounts](https://www.threerings.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2016/04/Managed-Accounts-300x88.png)](https://www.threerings.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2016/04/Managed-Accounts.png) Click the `My Account` link in the top right-hand corner and locate the line that says who your account is managed by. Select the [Change] link. **Optional:** Enter a name for the account. No-one else will see this name, and it will not appear anywhere apart from on the `My Account` page - it's just for your convenience. Enter an email address for your account. This **must** be an account you can access to if you need to reset your password. [alert\_box type="info" class="corners"] This email address can be completely different to the one listed on your directory page: it's only used for password resets, and won't affect messages sent via Comms.[/alert\_box] Click the `Convert Account` button. The system will then automatically send an e-mail to the account you supplied. You will need to open this email and click the link. Enter your password in the relevant field, click the `Convert Account` button, and your account page should now confirm that you are managing your account!

**Deleting your account.**

It is possible to delete your account, but it isn't a good idea! It will delete the top-level _Three Rings_ account but leave any organisation information untouched. If you want to leave one or more organisations, it is best to advise them so they can suspend your account and protect your data. If all of your accounts have been slept, you will be advised that you no longer volunteer anywhere and, if you wish, you can delete your _Three Rings_ account.

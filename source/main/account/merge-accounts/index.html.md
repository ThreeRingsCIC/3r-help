title: Merging Accounts
---

Merging two or more accounts lets you volunteer for multiple organisations using a single username and password. [alert\_box type="warning" class="corners"]To merge two accounts, they must both be self-managed accounts and have the same account email address.

[Convert your account to be self-managed](/help/account/converting-to-a-self-managed-account) | [Edit your account email address](/help/account/additional-options/)[/alert\_box]

To merge the two accounts, log in to the account you want to keep. After the accounts are merged, this is the account you'll log in to from now on.

1. Click the `Merge Accounts` link at the bottom-right of the `My Account` page. 
2. In the `This Account` box, enter the password **for the account you are currently logged in to**. 
3. Enter the username and password of the other account you wish to merge into this one then click the `Merge` button.

The other username and password will now stop working, and you can access your volunteer profiles for both organisations from one account.

[alert\_box type="info" class="corners"]You can do this multiple times to merge accounts; there's no limit on the number of different organisations' logins you can have in one account.[/alert\_box]

[alert\_box type="success" class="corners"]

#### Switching between profiles

You can change which volunteer profile / which organisation you're logged into at any time. Select `My Account` at the top-right corner of any page, and then log in to whichever organisation's profile you want to view. [/alert\_box]

#### Setting a Primary Login

You can also select a primary login, which is the organisation you will be logged into automatically each time you log in to _Three Rings_. On the right hand side of the `My Account` page you can see your primary login. To change this, click `Change` then select the account you want to be your primary login from the drop-down menu. If your primary account is set to "None", then when you log in to _Three Rings_ you'll see the `My Account` page instead of being logged in to one of your volunteer profiles.

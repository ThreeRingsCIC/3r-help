title: Additional Options for Self-Managed Accounts
---

Below are options on your `My Account` page that can **only** be altered by users who have elected to self-manage their accounts.

#### Change your username

Locate the username field on the right-hand side. Click the `Change username` button. Enter your new username in the username field and click the `Change username` button.

[alert\_box type="info"]You can't pick a username that's being used by anyone else - it will have to be unique among all _Three Rings_ users[/alert\_box]

#### Change your name

Locate the name field on the right-hand side and click the `change name` button. Enter the new name in the name field and click the `Change Name` button.

[alert\_box type="info" class="corners"]Your name in this context will only appear on the `My Account` page.[/alert\_box]

#### Change email address

Locate the email field on the right-hand side and click the `Change email` button. In the first field, enter your current email address, then click the `Change email` button.

[alert\_box type="info"] This will change the Account email address but not the email address associated with any of your organisations as shown on your Directory page.[/alert\_box]

#### Merge accounts

This process allows you to merge two accounts together, to be able to log in to more than one different organisation using a single username and password. [Click here for more information.](/help/account/merge-accounts)

#### Configure two-factor authentication

This is where you can set up two-factor authentication. _Three Rings_ supports authenticator apps (such as Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator), or Yubikey. You can also download or print backup codes in case you lose access to your main authentication method. Finally, you can decide when _Three Rings_ will require you to use your second factor.

[alert\_box type="info"]Organisations can require the use of two-factor authentication to access certain areas of the system, e.g. the Admin tab. If your organisation has activated this feature, you won't be able to access those parts of the system until you have set up two-factor authentication.[/alert\_box]

[alert\_box type="info"]If two-factor authentication is set up on your account, it will always be required to make any changes to your two-factor settings, or to access the privacy report.[/alert\_box]

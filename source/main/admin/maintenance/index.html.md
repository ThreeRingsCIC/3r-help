The Maintenance page was created to remind administrators of tasks that are may require attention. Maintenance Tasks will appear periodically as the situation causing them changes, but you can also manually check them by clicking the '`Check`' button in the Maintenance panel. The following situations cause Maintenance Task reminders to be displayed:

- There are no nominated Support People at your organisation!
- You have "Sleepers" whose data may need to be "purged" because they’ve been asleep for a very long time
- You have awake volunteers who have previously logged in, but not for more than a year: perhaps they should be asleep?
- A volunteer’s account has been automatically Locked because they requested a password reset from a self-managed account (and your organisation has chosen to lock their account until you can confirm they asked for the password reset themselves)
- Your invoice details need to be checked – make sure your organisation has the right information on turnover and contact details in Admin \> Invoicing
- We'd like to know your postal address (even if you're not invoiced): usually because we want to send you something like the annual Christmas card!
- Other – A generic task that can be created by Three Rings if it’s needed, for example to warn your organisation of an invoice which is overdue for payment

When you have a Maintenance Task, it may mean you should check your settings, in case changes need to be made. You can Dismiss a Maintenance Task by clicking the '`Dismiss`' button for that task: doing so will make the task go away again for a while (3-12 months depending on the task). If you've Dismissed a Maintenance Task by accident, or if you want to make sure that you've solved the problem correctly, you can click '`Check`' to make sure. Any unresolved Maintenance Tasks will reappear in the list.

[alert\_box type="warning"]Dismissing a Maintenance Task dismisses it for all administrators at your organisation, not just for you![/alert\_box]

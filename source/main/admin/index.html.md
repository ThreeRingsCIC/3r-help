The Admin tab allows your organisation to customise the behaviour of _Three Rings._ In particular, it allows you to turn features on and off, configure the Rota, and assign Roles and Permissions to specific volunteers.

## Volunteer Management Tools

- [Roles](../admin/roles/) Understanding how to Create and Edit Roles to give your volunteers extra permissions
- [Role Workflows](../admin/role-workflows/) Allowing non-admins to add or remove certain roles from other volunteers
- [New Volunteer](../directory/new/) Adding a new volunteer to _Three Rings_ and linking a volunteer's Individual Accounts
- [Import](../admin/import/) Creating new volunteer accounts in bulk

- [Properties](../admin/properties/) Enabling, disabling, and controlling access to the properties used in the Directory
- [Relationships and Inactivity](../admin/relationships-and-inactivity/) Defining Relationships between your volunteers and defining Inactivity types
- [What are Self-Managed Accounts?](../admin/what-are-self-managed-accounts/) Understanding what volunteers with Self-Managed Accounts can and can't do

## Rota Management Tools

- [Rotas](../admin/rotas/) Manage your organisation's Rotas and the permissions linked to them
- [Rules and Shifts](../admin/rules-and-shifts/) Setting rules governing what shifts volunteers can sign up to, and Adding, Editing & Deleting shifts
- [Closures](../admin/closures/) Creating and Editing bulk closure periods on your Rotas
- [Rota Automation](../admin/autopopulate-and-suggest-somebody/) Defining how Autopop and Suggest Somebody features should choose volunteers

[alert\_box type="danger" class="corners"]_Three Rings_ is not a source of legal advice. _Three Rings_ itself, and the Privacy tools and information on this page in particular, are designed to help you meet your legal obligations. However, this doesn't change the need for you to have robust data protection policies and procedures.[/alert\_box]

The Privacy section is designed to help you write and share the key elements of your organisation's Privacy Policy with your volunteers.

At the top of the page you can find important information about the role of this section and how to use it effectively. Please make sure to read this before continuing further.

[alert\_box type="warning" class="corners"]_Three Rings_ is based in the UK, so even if your organisation isn't, the data you store in _Three Rings_ still needs to conform to UK data protection rules.[/alert\_box]

The next section of the page shows your organisation's current privacy information, as it's displayed to your volunteers. This is divided into several sections: Purposes, Bases, Sharing, Retention, and Contact. Below this you will see the same five sections again, but this time with checkboxes and text boxes, where you can enter and edit this information. After making any changes, click the Save Changes button at the bottom of the page.

[alert\_box type="info" class="corners"]Remember that the information here is about your organisation's relationship with your volunteers (i.e. the people who log into _Three Rings_ and/or whose data you're storing within _Three Rings_. It's not about how you process your service users' data.[/alert\_box]

**Purposes**

The first section is about the reason(s) why you store and process your volunteers' personal data. Type the information into the text box, or if you prefer you could copy-and-paste this information from a separate Privacy Policy document if applicable.

**Bases**

Under data protection law, personal data can only be processed where there is one or more legal basis for doing so. These legal bases are: consent, contract, legal obligation, vital interests, public interest, or legitimate interests. Tick the checkbox(es) next to all that apply. There is also further guidance below each to help explain that legal basis, however if you are unsure you should always seek legal advice.

**Sharing**

If you share personal data with other organisations (e.g. statistical reports with an umbrella organisation or volunteer demographics with a recruitment agency), state these arrangements here. Enter the details into the text box.

**Retention**

This section covers how long you store and retain data for, in particular after they stop volunteering at your organisation.

**Contact**

This is where you should enter the details of the person or people that Three Rings CIC and/or your volunteers (past, present, or future) should contact with any privacy or data protection related questions, queries, or requests. This will normally be your Data Protection Officer (DPO) if you have one.

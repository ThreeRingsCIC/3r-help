This page allows you to disable and enable a wide variety of features in _Three Rings_. To enable a feature, tick the checkbox next to the it and then click `Save Changes`. To disable a feature, untick the box and `Save Changes` again.

**Core Features**

The first section allows you to activate or deactivate various core features of _Three Rings_: [Comms](/help/comms), the [Filestore](/help/filestore), and the [Wiki](/help/wiki). You can also choose to enable or disable the use of [Filestore Secrets](/help/filestore/secrets%20store/).

**Rota Enhancements**

These are optional features that offer additional functionality on the Rota:

- Swaps
- Confirmations
- Points
- Titles
- Whether or not to display inactivity icons in the Rota's Volunteer List
- Shift reminders
- Use starchart summaries

You can find more information about what each of these features does by reading its description on the Features page.

**Availability**

Enabling the Availability feature allows volunteers to specify days and times that they're available for shifts. This is then shown on the volunteer's Directory page, and used for generating availability reports or when using rota automation tools like Autopopulate or Suggest Somebody.

[alert\_box type="info" class="corners"]Adding availability doesn't stop volunteers from signing themselves up, or being signed up by rota managers, at times outside their specified availability.[/alert\_box]

**Rota Automation**

Rota automation allows the use of the Autopopulator tool to assist with allocating shifts on a managed rota, and the Suggest Somebody tool to make suggestions about who is likely to be able to fill a gap in the rota, for example to help prioritise who the best people might be to contact to try and fill the gap.

**Rota Priority Ratings**

Rota priority ratings, or "heatmap", highlight the shifts on the rota with the most urgent need. This was developed primarily for Samaritans branches but can be used by anyone. If you've not used this feature before, we recommend contacting _Three Rings_ support for details and instructions.

**User Interfaces**

You can enable or disable different user interfaces at your organisation. The **Simple interface** is designed for volunteers with extremely limited computer skills - many features are not accessible when using the Simple interface, and we recommend volunteers use the Full interface instead where possible. The **Accessible interface** is designed to make _Three Rings_ easier to use with the aid of a screen reader or braille pad - if you have volunteers that use these kinds of technology, we recommend using the Accessible interface.

**Birthdays**

If your organisation stores volunteers' dates of birth, you can choose to display birthdays, either on the Rota and/or in the Upcoming Events section of the Overview.

[alert\_box type="info" class="corners"]Birthdays will only be displayed to volunteers who have permissions to see that volunteer's date of birth in the Directory.[/alert\_box]

**Relationships**

Relationships are used to track links between volunteers, e.g. mentor/mentee relationships.

**System Logging**

In this section you can choose to show or hide certain types of information in the logs. The first option is to show or hide the content (message body) of emails and text messages sent via Comms. The second option is to show or hide logs from where iCalendar messages interact with _Three Rings_ where volunteers have signed up to have their shifts feed into their calendar, e.g. on their phone (usually, you will probably want to hide these messages).

[alert\_box type="info" class="corners"]Disabling these doesn't stop the information from being logged, it just means it isn't displayed.[/alert\_box]

## Upcoming Shifts

_Three Rings_ lists your upcoming shifts alongside other upcoming events. Depending how many other Events are taking place at your organisation, this might not show all your future shifts.

[![Upcoming Shifts and iCal](https://www.threerings.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2016/04/Upcoming-Shifts-and-iCal-300x121.png)](https://www.threerings.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2016/04/Upcoming-Shifts-and-iCal.png)

## Exporting Your Shifts to a Third-Party Calendar

You can export a list of all the shifts you're signed up to in _Three Rings_ for use with third-party calendar software such as Google Calendar or Microsoft Outlook. To do this, first click on the link `You have x upcoming shifts`, and then on the `Download/subscribe iCalendar` button. This will pop up a list of options which you can use to download or subscribe to the feed, depending on which calendar program you are using.

You can only subscribe to a calendar if Feed Keys are enabled by an Administrator in Admin \> Security.

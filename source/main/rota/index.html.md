The rota page lets you view rotas running at your organisation. It lets any volunteers with a role granting Rota Self Manage to sign themselves up to shifts, and lets users with Rota Manage permissions sign up others and make wider changes to the rota.

- [Viewing the Rota](../rota/viewing-the-rota/) Information on viewing the rota, changing rota view, and navigating to a specific date
- [Managing your shifts](../rota/managing-your-own-shifts-on-the-rota/) How to sign up to shifts, pull out of shifts, and handle shift swaps
- [Managing others' shifts](../rota/managing-other-peoples-shifts/) Information for Rota Managers and Admins
- [Rota Admin](../admin/rotas/) Help on Admin \> Rota Management Tools

### Signing up to Shifts

#### Signing a Volunteer up for a shift

Find the shift you want to sign a volunteer up for on the rota. Click `Sign up` on the shift, and then on the drop-down list of names in the pop-up box. Select the name of the volunteer that you want to sign up to the shift then click `Sign up`. [![Signing others up to shifts](https://www.threerings.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2016/04/Signing-others-up-to-shifts-300x185.png)](https://www.threerings.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2016/04/Signing-others-up-to-shifts.png)

**Signing up Volunteers for Recurring Shifts** [alert\_box type="info" class="corners"] If you do not have permission to sign a volunteer up for recurring shifts, the recurring shifts box will not be displayed [/alert\_box] Find the shift you want to sign the volunteer up for, and click `Sign up`. Select the volunteer you want to sign up from the drop-down box and check the `Tick to sign up to a series of recurring shift` box. Click `Sign up`, and on the next page, tick all the boxes against the shifts you want to sign the volunteer up for.

#### Signing more Volunteers up to a Shift

You can 'Overstuff' a shift to sign up more volunteers to a shift than would normally be allowed. Find the shift you want to sign a volunteer up for. Click on one of the names of the volunteers on the shift, and click on the drop-down box `Who would you like to sign up to this shift, with (names)`. Click on the name of the volunteer you want to sign up, and then click the `Sign up button`. In the pop-up box, if it appears, check the tick box labelled `Tick this box to override rules`, then click `Sign up`.

#### Signing up Multiple Volunteers for Shifts (Drag and Drop)

To sign multiple volunteers up for a shift, you can use the procedure outlined above, or you may find it more efficient to use the drag-and-drop rota facility. You can move a volunteer from one shift to another or from the Volunteer List. Click the `Volunteer list` button in the top right corner of the rota. A colour-coded list of volunteers will drop down. The numbers next to the volunteers' names shows how many shifts they're already signed up to in the current view you're in (week view, fortnight view or monthly view). To sign a volunteer up for a shift, drag their name onto the shift vacancy you want to sign them up for. [alert\_box type="info" class="corners"] To drag and drop, click and hold the left mouse button while the mouse cursor is on the volunteer's name, then move the mouse over to the shift, and release the mouse button. If you want to copy a volunteer to a new shift, press the CTRL key as you drag-and-drop. This will copy the volunteer to the new shift and leave them in their previous shift.[/alert\_box]

### Swapping Shifts

**Allowing a Volunteer to Swap into another's Shift** Swapping is one-way: you might allow someone to may swap into another volunteer's shift, but the replaced volunteer won't be automatically signed up to do one of their shifts in return. Find the shift you want to allow swapping on. Click on the name of the volunteer you wish to allow to swap out of the shift and then the `Allow another volunteer to swap into this shift` link in the pop-up box. **Stopping a Volunteer from Allowing Other's to Swap into their Shift** If you change your mind about letting other volunteers swapping into a shift, you can prevent them from doing so. Navigate to the shift you wish to prevent volunteers from swapping into, click on the volunteer's name on the shift and then click on the `Stop allowing other volunteers to swap into this shift` link. **Swapping another Volunteer into a Shift** Shifts that you are allowed to swap into have a yellow double-arrow symbol next to them. Click on the name of the volunteer on the shift you want to swap someone onto. From the pop-up box, click 'Sign someone up, replacing 'name''

### Overriding

**Overriding Rota Rules** If you are a user with Rota Manageme permissions, you can override the rota rules when required, for example pulling users out of shifts despite grace periods being exceeded. [![Overriding Shift Rules](https://www.threerings.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2016/04/Overriding-Shift-Rules-300x171.png)](https://www.threerings.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2016/04/Overriding-Shift-Rules.png) **Overriding Volunteer Availability** You may wish to sign up a volunteer to a shift that occurs during a period when they have defined themselves as unavailable. The system will produce a message notifying you of this fact. If you are sure you wish to sign up the selected volunteer to the shift, click the checkbox and then press the `Sign Up` button. [alert\_box type="info" class="corners"] Performing this action will not alter the volunteer's defined periods of unavailability. [/alert\_box] **Pulling a Volunteer out of a Shift** Find the shift you want to pull a volunteer out of, click on their name and then click `Pull them out of the shift`.

### Shift History

If you have the relevant permissions, the history of a shift, including who signed up, when and who dropped out can be viewed from the shift tab in the rota. Who can view the shift history is controlled on a per rota basis from the rota permissions. As default, only roles that have access to the Admin -\> Logs panel can view this information. You can also compile a report of shift histories for multiple roots and dates in Stats. [![Shift History](https://www.threerings.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2016/04/Shift-History-300x138.png)](https://www.threerings.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2016/04/Shift-History.png)

[alert\_box type="info" class="corners"]This page is about what to do if you've forgotten your username or your password. If you're an administrator and you'd like to help another volunteer who has forgotten their password, see [Resetting another volunteer's Password](/help/resetting-another-volunteers-password).[/alert\_box]

If you try to log in to _Three Rings_ with the wrong username or password, you will see a red error message which reads `Bad username/password combination.` **If you see another error message, something else is likely to be causing the problem.** In particular, if you enter an incorrect Username/Password combination several times, you may be asked to wait before trying again - this is a security feature to prevent 'brute force' attempts to break into _Three Rings_ by guessing lots and lots of passwords over and over.

## Forgotten Usernames

If you've forgotten your username but you know your password, try speaking to another volunteer at your organisation: most organisations follow a pattern or theme in their usernames - if you know another volunteer's username, perhaps you'll be able to work out what yours is? If not, you'll need to speak to an administrator at your organisation: these people often have Roles with names like Director, Manager, Coordinator, Chairman, Three Rings Champion, and so on. They'll be able to log in to _Three Rings_ and tell you what your username is. If it helps, you can keep your username written down somewhere safe.

## Forgotten Passwords

If you've forgotten your password, you'll need to get in touch with the relevant Support Person at your organisation: these people often have job titles like Director, Coordinator, Chairman, Three Rings Champion, or IT Support Officer. They'll be able to log into Three Rings and issue you with an temporary password. [alert\_box type="info" class="corners"]Users with a self-managed account should be able to reset their own password using the Forgotten Password link on the login page - their Organisation won't be able to do it for them. See the [Self-Managed Accounts page](/help/admin/what-are-self-managed-accounts/) for more information.[/alert\_box] Log in using your usual username and your temporary password, [just as if you were logging in for the first time](/help/logging-in-for-the-first-time). If it doesn't work, double-check that you're using the correct username. If your username contains spaces or punctuation, take special care to ensure that these are in the correct place. You'll be asked to choose a new password. Once you've done this, your temporary password will be "used up" and will no longer work. You shouldn't write down your password, but if you must write it down in order to remember it, it's better to try and use a dedicated Password Manager programme, which will keep it (and any other passwords!) safely encrypted for you. If you're writing it down in plain text, don't write it anywhere that it can be associated with your Three Rings account (so don't write it next to your username!), and you must keep it safe (for example, in your wallet or purse). Consider writing down only the first half of your password, and memorising the second half. If you lose a written-down password, you should change your password as soon as possible.

## Getting help from Three Rings volunteers

[alert\_box type="warning" class="corners"]Under normal circumstances, **Three Rings staff will not help you if you have forgotten your username or password** : you must speak to the relevant support person at your organisation.[/alert\_box] However, if you are the **only** administrator capable of sending new passwords out, and you've forgotten your password, Three Rings staff can make arrangements to give you access to your account. This will involve phoning or posting a letter to your organisation's official phone number or address, in order to validate the legitimacy of your intentions. Please [contact us](https://www.threerings.org.uk/contact) to discuss this.
